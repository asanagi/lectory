Why We Founded Lectory

The Disconnect in Enterprise Training

Every year, global enterprises spend over $380 billion on workplace training, compliance certifications, and leadership coaching. Yet despite massive investment, most employees retain less than 20% of what they watch in standard LMS video modules after 30 days.

When high-stakes situations happen—a manager delivering hard performance feedback, a salesperson handling an aggressive pricing objection, or an employee witnessing workplace harassment—passive video training fails to prepare them for the emotional reality of spoken conversation.

Lectory was founded to close this gap. We provide a safe, scalable simulation environment where every employee can practice difficult business conversations with emotionally responsive AI avatars until they achieve genuine fluency.

Our Guiding Principles

Values That Drive How We Build

The fundamental beliefs that guide our engineering, product architecture, and enterprise partnerships.

Practice Over Passivity

Human beings build confidence through conversational trial, error, and repetition. We prioritize experiential simulations over passive multimedia lectures.

Velocity Without Bottlenecks

When business policies or market conditions change, learning materials must update in seconds. We eliminate camera crews, studio rentals, and video re-renders.

Uncompromising Data Sovereignty

Enterprise training data, internal documents, and learner audio streams belong solely to the customer. We maintain a strict zero-retention foundation model policy.
