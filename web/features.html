Pillar 01

1. Real-Time Spoken Avatar Simulation

Learners engage in natural, two-way spoken conversation with emotionally responsive AI avatars. Unlike scripted branch trees with multiple-choice buttons, Lectory’s voice-to-voice loop allows employees to practice real speaking skills in a safe sandbox.

  • Sub-Second Voice Loop: Low-latency WebRTC streaming audio with instantaneous conversational turn-taking.
  • Adaptive Emotional Reactivity: Avatars react dynamically with micro-expressions and vocal intonation matching learner tone.
  • Unscripted Branching: Scenarios adapt realistically based on how well the learner uses empathy, de-escalation, and policy logic.
Pillar 02

2. No-Code Course Studio & Instant Policy Ingest

Turn static PDF employee handbooks, compliance SOPs, and manager playbooks into fully interactive branching simulations in minutes. Instructional designers author prompt rules and grading rubrics without writing code or hiring video production crews.

  • Document Ingest Engine: Upload compliance PDFs, Word documents, or Notion guides to generate scenario trees.
  • <10s Instant Updates: Modify legal policies or company procedures and deploy updates instantly with zero re-rendering queues.
  • Visual Scenario Node Graph: Configure personas, objectives, hints, and escalation thresholds in a clean visual canvas.
Pillar 03

3. Automated Rubric Grading & Compliance Audit Trails

Eliminate subjective human grading bottlenecks. Lectory’s automated rubric engine scores every conversational simulation against objective organizational standards, providing instant personalized coaching to learners and audit-proof records to compliance officers.

  • Objective Multi-Criteria Scoring: Evaluates factual compliance accuracy, empathy, tone of voice, and resolution speed.
  • Turn-by-Turn Transcript Replay: Learners see verbatim dialogue with inline highlight cards suggesting improved phrasing.
  • Audit-Ready Compliance Export: 1-click CSV and webhook sync to satisfy OSHA, EEOC, FINRA, and internal audit requirements.
