Trust & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

Last updated: August 23, 2026. How Lectory ensures strict data isolation, zero AI model training retention, and edge compliance.

Zero-Retention AI Commitment: Lectory does not use customer scenario prompts, uploaded policies, employee voice recordings, or simulation scores to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundational AI models.

1. Overview & Information We Collect

Lectory, Inc. ("Lectory", "We", "Us") respects the privacy and confidentiality of enterprise employees, administrators, and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices across our marketing domain (lectory.dev), web application player (app.lectory.dev), and edge APIs.

We process the following categories of information:

  • Account & Directory Information: Corporate email addresses, full names, roles, and SSO identifiers provided during account provisioning.
  • Customer Authoring Data: Scenario prompts, compliance policies, instructional rubrics, and organizational guidelines entered into the authoring wizard.
  • Simulation Interaction Payloads: Real-time audio streams (ephemeral voice input during simulations), text chat inputs, turn-by-turn state graphs, and automated scorecard evaluations.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser user agent strings, and edge performance metrics required for network routing, security, and WAF geofencing.

2. AI Model Training & Ephemeral Processing Guarantees

2.1 Zero Model Training: All interactions with large language models (LLMs), speech-to-text (STT) decoders, and text-to-speech (TTS) neural synthesizers are processed under zero-data-retention commercial API agreements. Customer inputs and avatar responses are discarded by inference providers immediately following real-time turn generation.

2.2 Local Browser Rendering: 3D avatar meshes, procedural lip-sync visemes, and facial expressions are synthesized locally on the learner's device via WebGL. Raw video streams are neither transmitted nor stored on external media servers.

3. Enterprise Security & Trust Center

Lectory is built from the ground up for the security and compliance requirements of global enterprises:

3.1 Encryption Standards

  • In Transit: All web traffic and real-time audio streams are encrypted using TLS 1.3 with strict HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security) enabled.
  • At Rest: Scenario state databases, assessment logs, and customer metadata are encrypted using AES-256 with tenant-level key separation.

3.2 Identity, SSO & Access Controls

  • Enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) integration via SAML 2.0 and OIDC (Okta, Microsoft Azure AD, Google Workspace, Ping Identity).
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) separating Authors, Cohort Administrators, and Learners.
  • Audit log generation for scenario authoring, cohort dispatch, and grading exports.

3.3 Cloudflare Edge WAF & Geofencing

  • Cloudflare Workers edge deployment with sub-second asset distribution and DDoS mitigation.
  • Custom Edge WAF rules enforcing jurisdiction geofencing and rate-limiting against unauthorized automated bots.

4. Third-Party Sub-processors

Lectory engages vetted, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certified sub-processors strictly for infrastructure hosting and real-time inference routing:

Sub-processor Service Purpose Data Location / Safeguards
Cloudflare, Inc. Edge CDN, DNS, WAF Geofencing & Email Routing Global Edge (Zero-Retention Routing)
Amazon Web Services (AWS) / GCP Cloud Infrastructure & Encrypted State Storage United States / European Union (AES-256)
Enterprise AI Inference Partners Real-time STT, LLM Turn Generation & TTS Synthesis US / EU (Zero Model Training Agreement)

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable global data privacy regulations:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Enterprise administrators can export all scenario graphs, rubrics, and learner scores via 1-click SCORM / CSV export at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: Upon contract termination or formal deletion request, all tenant data and database records are permanently purged within 30 days.
